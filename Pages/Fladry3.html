Fladry

Effective against
✓ Large canid
✓ Small canid
✕ Large cat

Fladry is a barrier made using long strips of fabric tied to a string or wire. The strips of fabric flutter in the wind.

Predators avoid crossing fladry fences because they do not want to touch something that is new or that moves strangely.

The longer fladry is left out, the longer predators have time to test the fladry and learn it will not hurt them.

Eventually predators will lose their fear and learn to ignore fladry.

Therefore, fladry should not be used for longer than 30 to 75 days at a time.

However, predators will forget and fladry can be placed out the next year. Therefore, Fladry is most effective when it is put up only when livestock are most vulnerable such as in spring.

Design and Materials

Flags should be suspended about 50cm off the ground. Gap size should be small enough that the predator cannot cross without touching the flags either under or between.

Many animals, including most predators and horses, cannot see the color red. This is why, even though a tiger is bright to us humans, they are well camouflaged from their prey.

While red can help other people see your fladry barrier, consider also using blue, yellow, and purple to make it more visible to predators.
