Fladry

Effectiveness

✓ Large canid
✓ Small canid
✕ Large cat

Fladry is a barrier made using long strips of fabric tied to a string or wire. The moving flags can temporarily frighten predators and discourage them from crossing the boundary.

Design and Materials

Flags should be suspended about 50cm off the ground Gap size: small enough that the predator cannot cross without touching the flags either under or between.
